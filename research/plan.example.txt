#   - the setting is what goes after "pci=<address>;" in NVreg_RegistryDwordsPerDevice (several keys: KEY=VALUE;KEY=VALUE)
#   - commands: "lgc=min,max" / "lmc=min,max" lock the GPU / memory clock during the load ; "idle" = idle-only probe (~25 s)
#   - consecutive lines with the SAME setting do not reload the driver (they are sub-measurements)
